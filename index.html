Dew Drops

Dew drops can be collected from healthy buds and plants at the start of each day.

Buds reward 1 drop per day

Flowers reward 2 drops per day and a bonus on the day after watering your flower.

Dead plants subtract 2 from your dew drops.

Dew drops are reset every day, and can never go below 0.
